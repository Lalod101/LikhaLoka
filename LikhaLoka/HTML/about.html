About Us

LikhaLoka promotes Filipino artisans and their handmade products. We start with crafts from Western Visayas like hablon, piña and woven bags.

Our Vision

Every local artisan is seen, and every handmade product is valued.

Our Mission

Connect artisans with customers who support Filipino-made products.
